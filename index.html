About

I am a Systems Engineer at NSSOL Okinawa and a Visiting Researcher at the University of the Ryukyus. My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and network systems, with a focus on traffic forecasting and anomaly detection.

I received my Master of Engineering degree in Intelligent Information from the University of the Ryukyus in September 2025. During my master's studies, I investigated multi-output Gaussian process models that use correlations among multiple network traffic signals to improve prediction accuracy and identify anomalous behavior.

Network Traffic Forecasting
